MEET THE CEO

Prasad Mandava

Chief Executive Officer, VTalent

A Vision to Empower Telugu Talent and Transform Careers

With around 15 years of professional experience, including 10 years in India and 3 years in the United States, Prasad Mandava brings valuable industry knowledge, leadership experience, and a strong vision for transforming the careers of aspiring professionals.

Driven by a passion for empowering Telugu students, he envisioned creating opportunities for students across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana to enter the IT industry through quality training, practical skills, and access to top career opportunities.

With this vision, he established VTalent in 2014, with a mission to bridge the gap between academic education and industry expectations. His goal was not just to provide training but to prepare students with the technical expertise, professional confidence, and industry-ready skills required to build successful careers.

A Journey of Impact

Under his leadership and vision, VTalent has worked towards transforming thousands of student careers, achieving a significant milestone of 3,000+ students placed in the IT industry by 2024.

This achievement reflects his commitment to creating meaningful employment opportunities and helping aspiring graduates turn their dreams into reality.

His Vision for the Future

His ultimate vision is to help every Telugu student get an opportunity to build a successful career in the IT industry.

He believes that every student deserves access to quality technical training, real-time project experience, industry guidance, and opportunities with top companies, regardless of their educational background or financial circumstances.

Through VTalent, he continues to work towards building a skilled, confident, and industry-ready generation of Telugu professionals.

"Our mission is not just to train students, but to transform their potential into successful IT careers. We want to see Telugu talent represented across the global IT industry."

— Prasad Mandava, CEO, VTalent
